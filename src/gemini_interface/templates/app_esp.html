ESP Pump Curve and Real-Time Data

Note:
The monitoring tab shows real-time data. If you observe a mismatch between the real-time frequency and the operational point on the pump curve with the theoretical frequency, the ESP might not be calibrated correctly. In this case, please go to the Calibration tab and calibrate the calculated pump head.

Calibration Setup

Calibration Process:
The calibration matches the linear fitting to the calculated pump head. We try to match the calculated VLP head to the theoretical head that comes from the pump curve.
