Heat-pump upgrade (37.78 [R0220]), absorption cooling (40.64 [R0026]), pit store (36.63 [R0224]), ORC (38.97 [R0102]), electric boiler (35.65 [R0273]) and heat-recovery HP (35.96 [R0274]) all lose on cost. GEMINI's own Solver agrees with the dispatcher on the peak-heating day [R0272].

**Design space searched:** ~250 designs across 1–3 doublets, 1500–3500 m, boiler vs heat pump, electric vs absorption, all four chiller types, boiler 14–34 MW in 1–6 units, chiller 8–12 MW, ORC 0–3 MW, electric boiler, heat-recovery HP, pit store 50–100 k m³, ESP sizing/drive, return temperatures 32–45 / 24–28 °C, outage timing, and operating rules.

**Two caveats you should know:**
1. **It is cheap but not robust.** Under GEMINI's stress cases it fails `boiler_unit_out` (5,029 MWh unmet), `trip_at_peak` (5,900 MWh) and `demand_high` (+10 %: 192 MWh heat, 93 MWh cooling) [R0339, R0338, R0337]. The 16 MW boiler only covers peak-minus-geothermal. A 30 MW boiler (2 × 15 MW) passes `trip_at_peak` but still fails `boiler_unit_out` (32.5 MWh unmet) and `demand_high` (93 MWh cooling), at 36.45 €/MWh [R0270, R0343, R0344, R0342].
2. **A 2-doublet alternative** at 2900 m costs 36.33 €/MWh but cuts CO₂ from 10,190 to 5,582 t/yr and survives `trip_at_peak`, `boiler_unit_out` and `demand_high`; only `combined` still fails (128 MWh heat) [R0320, R0326, R0327, R0347, R0348].

**Assumptions used:** heating return 40 °C, cooling return 28 °C (both design choices, priced against the 40/26 °C reference), each doublet's 35-day stop in its own lowest-load window (leaving `outage_start_days` unset — forcing `-1` explicitly gives 36.55 [R0219]), NL 2027 prices (gas 40 €/MWh, power 120 €/MWh, CO₂ 80 €/t), assumed cost curves, no weather data (so free cooling and ATES were not usable). LCOE is sensitive to prices: 31.07 €/MWh at 20 €/MWh gas and 42.75 at 80 [R0282, R0285]; 28.95 €/MWh at zero CO₂ price and 40.99 at 160 €/t [R0286, R0288].
