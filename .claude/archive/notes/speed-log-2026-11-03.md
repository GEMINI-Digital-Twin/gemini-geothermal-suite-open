# gemini speed log

Measured on the NTNU Dell (32 threads), quiet machine unless noted.
- Per-trial CPU: `study.json` on HeatingCoolingPlant alone, serial, 240 trials, process CPU time.
- hackathon.py: `examples/hackathon.py`, 8 processes, wall time; output must stay byte-identical.
- Fast tier: `pytest -n 16 -q`, wall time.

| Version | hackathon.py wall | study CPU (240 trials) | per trial | fast tier | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5.6 | 3:29 (loaded) | ~37 s (loaded) | ~150 ms | - | baseline before speed work |
| 0.5.7 | 2:11 (loaded) | ~26 s | ~110 ms | - | frame build, column reads, tree walk |
| 0.5.8 | - | -14% CPU vs 0.5.7 (loaded) | - | - | table in one step, balances read arrays |
| 0.5.10 | 1:32 | 20.8 s | 87 ms | 36 s | quiet baseline for feature/speed |
| 0.5.11 | 1:29 | 19.9 s | 83 ms | 33 s | roles read once per schedule, verdict and check (-4% CPU) |

Next targets (profile of 0.5.11, study of one plant, 240 trials):
- tree walks spread over many readers (`leaf_total`, `outputs()`, `controls()`, `apply_case`): about 10%.
- `copy.deepcopy` of the plant per trial (study/_utils/shared clone, apply): about 6%.
- the interpreter's `_first` and `propagate` in the compiled replay: about 5%.
- `pandas` column access in evaluate (`leaf_total` sums via a frame): about 5%.

Tried after 0.5.11, no measurable gain (dropped):
- caching the leaf list of `leaf_total` per evaluation: 19.97 s vs 19.9 s; the cost is the pandas sums, not the walk.
- skipping error-free operands in the interpreter's `propagate`: 20.2 s vs 19.9 s.
- pandas `attrs` deep copy: already shared (`_ColumnUnits.__deepcopy__`); about 380 small copies per trial, under 1%.
Stopped here: what remains is spread over many small costs, each under about 1%. The next real step
would be structural (fewer pandas tables in evaluate, or running trials of a batch through the
compiled graph at once) and needs a design decision first.
