# 1. Competition needs

## 1.1 Inputs and hard constraints

- ✅ Daily heating and cooling demand from CSV, checked against the `time` range
- ✅ Reservoir: reference depth, thickness, permeability, pressure; temperature from the gradient, pressure at another depth from a brine column
- ✅ Fluid: density, viscosity, gas-to-liquid ratio, gas composition (CH4, CO2)
- ✅ Associated gas: separator, blending with grid gas for the boiler, flare
- ✅ 1 to 3 doublets and the depth range in `choices`
- ✅ Flow rate 50 to 300 cubic meters per hour while running, brine cooling at most 40 kelvin, pump efficiency 70%, equal production and injection volume, 330 operating days: all as constraints on the `doublet` role, both in schedule and in the check
- ✅ Supply temperature 100 and 20 degrees, checked from `demand` itself
- ✅ Daily energy and mass balance

## 1.2 Challenge 2 outputs

- ✅ Capacity of each unit and peak use (`evaluate.capacities`)
- ✅ Single report: energy, capacities, capex of each module, opex, LCTS/LCOH/LCOC, CO2, all constraints, failed days (`evaluate.report`)
- ✅ Direct CO2, methane with GWP, and grid electricity
- 🟡 Costs: capex exists for 15 equipment types, but **the injection pump has no price** 🔴, and all prices and factors are placeholders with no source 🔴

## 1.3 Design and comparison

- ✅ Initial sizing (`study.size`): number of doublets, flow rate, capacity of the boiler, heat pump and chiller
- 🟡 Optimization (`study.minimize`) works on the number of doublets (discrete) and depth, but:
  - **it does not respect the case constraints** 🔴: in today's run, the optimal designs of `HeatingCoolingPlant` and `HeatingFullCoolingPlant` violate the cooling supply temperature constraint
  - **its result is worse than the manual design** 🔴: all five size+minimize designs are more expensive than the manual `HackathonPlant` (57.05 euros per megawatt-hour; the rest 58 to 67)
  - `HackathonPlant` is not searched at all, because it has no structural `doublets` size (`invalid_design`)
- 🟡 Comparison (`evaluate.compare`) gives a ranking and the Pareto front, but:
  - a design that violated a constraint or has no cooling (`HeatingPlant`) also gets ranked 🔴
  - its table is in SI (euros per joule, kilograms) and is not readable for a report 🟠
- ✅ One-at-a-time sensitivity (`study.sensitivity`); its output is also SI 🟠
- ✅ Uncertainty (`study.sample`): 20 samples in 11 seconds
- ✅ List of capabilities (`plants.capabilities`): roles, whether schedulable, choices of each plant

## 1.4 Speed

- 🟡 `hackathon.py` took 7 minutes and 22 seconds on this version 🔴
- The `speed` branch (daily year from about 8 to 0.14 seconds) is not finished and merged yet
- `hackathon.py` was removed from the backend parity test, because on the Python backend it went past 900 seconds

# 2. General design rules

- ✅ Strict `gm.Case`: an unknown key is an error with a suggestion of the correct name
- ✅ No competition name or number in `gemini/`; the competition only in `inputs/examples/hackathon_2027/`
- ✅ Site, fluid, weather and return temperature values moved out of the plant, and gemini applies them by module type
- ✅ A unit string (`"2500 m"`) is rejected everywhere; ‏`Quantity` and `Unit` ‏enum exist
- 🟡 **The unit rule is not complete**: `set_params`, `gm.call`, `gm.Stream` and `gm.Fluid` still take raw SI numbers (checked: `set_params("d0", {"well_diameter": 0.2})` is accepted)
- 🟡 **The no-default rule is not complete**: 34 module parameters still have a `default`. Some are physical constants and are correct (latent heat of ice, atmospheric pressure), but some are design decisions and must go: circulation pump efficiency, pipe roughness, ESP motor efficiency, well U factor, `Fluid.salinity` and so on. The cleanup report counted 8 items as definitely design decisions
- 🟡 `fluids` for a plant without a case still stays in the plant dictionary; consumer return pressure is still a plant parameter
- 🟡 The roles table is smaller than agreed: `heat_output` (heat pump, boiler, CHP), `cold_output` (chiller) and the `plant` role (bought electricity and gas, CO2, surplus) were removed
- ✅ The rule "update the code, do not write a wrapper" in the docs, with four quality tests
- ✅ 15 bugs fixed in the cleanup, each with a test

# 3. Not done, in order of priority

## 🔴 Blocks the competition

1. `minimize` must respect the case constraints (violating design = rejected)
2. size + minimize must at least match the manual design; find the cause (sizing, too few evaluations, or missing variables such as heat pump capacity)
3. `compare` should leave a violating or incomplete design out of the ranking and the Pareto front, and its table should have readable units
4. Injection pump price
5. Cost prices and factors with sources, before the report
6. Merge `speed` (`hackathon.py` time from 7 minutes to a few seconds)

## 🟠 Competition bonus

8. Failure cause diagnosis: summary of failed days by module and error code (today only raw `results.errors()`)
9. Multi-objective optimization (cost and CO2); today only `compare` gives the front after the run
10. Several units of one type in schedule (for example two cascaded heat pumps)
11. Storage and ATES in schedule, and carrying state between steps (`scan`)
12. Multi-year run with reservoir temperature decline
13. Two physical claims without a source: Arpagaus (10 to 80 kelvin) and `lorenz_fraction`

## ⚪ General, for later

14. Completing the unit rule in `set_params`, `gm.call`, `Stream`, `Fluid`
15. Removing design defaults from modules (about 8 to 34 items, by editing the plant classes)
16. Putting `heat_output`, `cold_output` and the `plant` role back into the roles table
17. `data.synthesize` (profile from peak or annual consumption)
18. Four well and corrosion analyses: `injectivity`, `calibrate_pump`, `calibrate_corrosion`, `erosion`
19. `plants.example` for the template classes (they have no values with sources)
20. The `choices` discussion (we agreed on later)
21. `C++` only if needed after `speed`
22. Two tests close to the time limit (`test_doublets`, `test_size`)
