- Run: `S5` · phase P5 · role system_architect
- Decision source: **llm**
- Question: Which cooling technology mix supplies 20 degC?
- **Choice: free-cooling-electric** — Free cooling + electric chillers (C2, C5)
- Decisive criterion: lowest lcoh_per_mwh + lcoc_per_mwh among guard-passed candidates without unmet demand in economics (key `lcoh_per_mwh` from economics; evidence TR-013, TR-014, TR-015, TR-016, TR-018, TR-019)
