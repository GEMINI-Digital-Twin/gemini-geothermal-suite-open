# DR-03 Heat top-up technology

- Run: `S11` · phase P5 · role system_architect
- Decision source: **llm**
- Question: Which technology lifts heat to the 100 degC supply?
- **Choice: hthp-series** — Plate HEX + HTHP in series + CHP + peak boiler (C2/C3)
- Decisive criterion: lowest lcoh_per_mwh + lcoc_per_mwh among guard-passed candidates without unmet demand in economics (key `lcoh_per_mwh` from economics; evidence TR-012, TR-013)
- Why: Selected because it achieved the lowest lcoh_per_mwh among guard-passed options in economics as evidenced by TR-013. gas-boiler-chp-topup was rejected for higher lcoh_per_mwh. deeper-direct-hex was rejected due to design_guard failure. shallow-large-hp was rejected for higher lcoh_per_mwh.
