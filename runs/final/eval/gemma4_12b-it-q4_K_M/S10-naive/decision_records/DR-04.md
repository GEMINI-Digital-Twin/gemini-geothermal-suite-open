# DR-04 Cooling technology mix

- Run: `S10-naive` · phase P5 · role system_architect
- Decision source: **llm**
- Question: Which cooling technology mix supplies 20 degC?
- **Choice: free-cooling-electric** — Free cooling + electric chillers (C2, C5)
- Decisive criterion: lowest lcoh_per_mwh + lcoc_per_mwh among guard-passed candidates without unmet demand in economics (key `lcoh_per_mwh` from economics; evidence TR-012, TR-013)
- Why: The selection is based on the ranking-lowest-lcoh_per_mwh criterion, where free-cooling-electric is the best-ranked option among candidates that satisfy all requirements without unmet demand as shown in TR-013. electric-chillers was rejected for having a higher lcoh_per_mwh. free-cooling-absorption was rejected because its candidate C4-2d was excluded by the design guard.
