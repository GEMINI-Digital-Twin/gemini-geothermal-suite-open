# DR-04 Cooling technology mix

- Run: `final_llm` · phase P5 · role system_architect
- Decision source: **llm**
- Question: Which cooling technology mix supplies 20 degC?
- **Choice: free-cooling-electric** — Free cooling + electric chillers (C2, C5)
- Decisive criterion: lowest lcoh_per_mwh + lcoc_per_mwh among guard-passed candidates without unmet demand in economics (key `lcoh_per_mwh` from economics; evidence TR-011, TR-012, TR-013, TR-015, TR-016)
- Why: Selected via criterion ranking-lowest-lcoh_per_mwh. This criterion identifies free-cooling-electric as the best option among guard-passed candidates with no unmet demand, per economics evidence TR-011 through TR-016.

| Option | Label | Eligible | Recommended | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| electric-chillers | Electric chillers (C1) | yes | no | TR-009, TR-010, TR-011, TR-012, TR-013, TR-015, TR-016 |
| free-cooling-electric | Free cooling + electric chillers (C2, C5) | yes | yes | TR-009, TR-010, TR-011, TR-012, TR-013, TR-015, TR-016 |
| free-cooling-absorption | Free cooling + absorption + electric peak (C3, C4) | yes | no | TR-009, TR-010, TR-011, TR-012, TR-015, TR-016 |

Rejected options:
- electric-chillers: Not the best under ranking-lowest-lcoh_per_mwh. It is best for lowest-lcoc_per_mwh, which is not the decisive criterion.
- free-cooling-absorption: Not the best under ranking-lowest-lcoh_per_mwh. Candidate C4 was excluded by design guard, and C3 did not rank highest.
