5.1.15.1. gemini_model.well.correlation

Well pressure drop correlation models.

Contains implementations of various correlations for calculating pressure drops in wellbores: - Beggs-Brill: multiphase flow correlation - Hagedorn-Brown: vertical multiphase flow - Darcy-Weisbach: friction factor correlation - Techo: temperature drop correlation

Modules

beggsbrill

Beggs–Brill two-phase pressure drop correlation.

darcyweisbach

Darcy–Weisbach single-phase pressure drop with Swamee–Jain friction factor.

temperaturedrop

Simple temperature drop model using an overall heat transfer coefficient.
