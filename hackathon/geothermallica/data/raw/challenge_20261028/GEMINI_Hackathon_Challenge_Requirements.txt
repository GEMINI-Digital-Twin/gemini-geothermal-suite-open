                                                                                                    GEMINI / AGENTIC GEOTHERMAL DESIGN HACKATHON




Required Inputs for Hackathon
Use agentic AI and the GEMINI geothermal digital twin to select, size and validate a deep-geothermal system serving an annual
heating and cooling load. The design must satisfy explicit thermal, hydraulic and operational constraints and explain every major
engineering decision.


1 Objective and scope
Participants must build an agentic AI orchestration workflow that uses GEMINI for applicable subsurface/well-engineering evaluations
and complementary component models for heating and cooling. The agent must interpret the provided demand profiles, generate
system configurations, run engineering checks, detect violations, and iteratively improve a proposed design.

GEMINI repository (development branch):

https://github.com/GEMINI-Digital-Twin/gemini-geothermal-suite-open/tree/development
Scope clarification: the autonomous system-level design agent is to be built by participants; do not assume that GEMINI already contains an
end-to-end heating-and-cooling optimizer.

Each file contains 365 daily-average power values for 1 January to 31 December 2027. Heating peaks on 28 January; cooling peaks on
19 July. Profiles are synthetic and include seasonal variation and short demand excursions. Convert a day’s power (MW) to daily
energy (MWh) by multiplying by 24 hours.
