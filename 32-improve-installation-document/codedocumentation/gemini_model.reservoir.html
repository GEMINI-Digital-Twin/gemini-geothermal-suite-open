5.1.6. gemini_model.reservoir

Reservoir models.

Contains simple models for IPR, injectivity, reservoir pressure estimation, and pressure drop calculations used in Gemini workflows.

Modules

inflow_performance

Inflow performance relationship (IPR) model.

injectivity_index

Injectivity index calculator.

reservoir_pressure_estimation

Reservoir pressure estimation from slope plot.

reservoir_pressuredrop

Bottomhole and reservoir pressure drop calculations with skin and hydrostatic.
