# Strong points and evidence

What sets this submission apart, with the file that proves each point. Paths are relative
to `hackathon_work/`. Numbers are from the committed outputs (rules backend, final pick
`n2_q300_s1.8_ua1000_z3500_co2ri`: 2 doublets at 3 500 m, 117 °C reservoir, CO₂ separated and
reinjected, CH₄ to CHP).

**Headline:** 3 983 t CO₂/yr, 85.1% below the conventional reference (gas boiler + electric
chiller, 26 706 t). External gas falls from 124.1 GWh to 0.016 GWh. Every day of 2027 is
served at 100 °C heating and 20 °C cooling, and all four judges' agent-reasoning tests pass.

## Honest limits

- Backup capacity is unlimited by construction, so unmet demand is 0. Peaks are daily averages, not hourly.
- Drilling cost and capex/opex are not modelled, which is why the depth rule is conservative.
- Free gas at the ESP intake is not modelled, and brine properties are extrapolated above 100 °C. Reinjected-gas dissolution is checked with a DERIVED correlation (about ±10% for CO₂); injection mixing, dip-tube hydraulics and reservoir interaction are not modelled.
- At 3 500 m the wellhead reaches 114 °C, beyond the 100 °C range of the constant brine-property model, so properties are extrapolated. Three sensitivity cases sit at the 5% depth/doublet thresholds, so the depth (3 250–3 500 m) and doublet count (2–3) are close calls.
- The claude_cli run (same pick) never proposed a flow above the cap, so test (ii) is not triggered there; the rules run triggers all four. The interactive run predates gas handling.
