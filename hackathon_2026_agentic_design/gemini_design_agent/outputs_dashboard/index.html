Why depth matters

At the specified 2,500 m the brine reaches the surface at about 85 C, below the 100 C supply, so all heat must be lifted by a heat pump. From about 3,300 m the wellhead clears 103 C and the plate exchanger supplies 100 C directly, while the absorption chiller runs at full COP. Hotter than that does not help: the 40 C brine-cooling cap, not the temperature, fixes the heat each doublet can deliver.

Real-time ESP smart monitoring

Hourly telemetry of one producer ESP is compared with the GEMINI-based twin at every sample. A robust Mahalanobis detector and an Isolation Forest watch the residuals, a CUSUM watches the hydraulic-efficiency drift, and a signature classifier names the fault. The health index feeds the 25-year loop as the ESP efficiency factor. Telemetry is synthetic (no field data in the challenge) with four injected faults the detector was not told about.

Component models built for this challenge

GEMINI has no heating or cooling plant, so these transfer functions are ours. They are deliberately simple and transparent; every design decision above traces back to one of them.

Heat-pump COP vs brine inlet temperature

Sink 100 C supply; COP = 0.5 x Carnot with 5 C pinches, capped at 8. Below about 20 C brine the lift exceeds the 85 C envelope.

Absorption-chiller COP vs driving temperature

Single-effect LiBr: off below 72 C, ramps to the nominal 0.72 at 95 C. The dashed line is the selected design's wellhead temperature.

Bubble point vs gas-liquid ratio

Henry-type estimate for 80 % CH4 / 20 % CO2. At the specified GLR of 0.8 Nm3/m3 the brine degasses below about 29 bar, which sets the ESP depth.

Wellhead temperature vs depth and the direct-supply threshold

12 C surface + 30 C/km, less about 3 C wellbore loss. Direct 100 C supply needs 103 C at the wellhead (3 C heat-exchanger approach): the cliff near 3,250 m.

External optimisers on the same twin

The continuous design variables were handed to black-box optimisers through petlab: DFTR-Filter (py-trsqp), COBYLA, and StoSAG robust optimisation over a geological ensemble. Every candidate was re-checked at full daily fidelity.
