5.1.1.4.1. gemini_model.corrosion.correlation.co2_partial_pressure_model

CO2 partial pressure helper model.

This module provides CO2PartialPressureModel to estimate the partial pressure of CO2 in an aqueous system given gas composition and conditions. It computes dissolved and gaseous CO2 contributions and applies Henry’s law at system temperature.
